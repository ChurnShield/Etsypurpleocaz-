# Prompt Library

Plain English prompts for Andy. No IDs, no technical details — CC reads the skill files for those.

---

## Build a new listing

> Build a new Etsy listing for [product name] in the [niche] niche. Here's the title: "[title]". Here's the description: "[paste description]". Here are the tags: [paste tags]. Use the standard price, quantity, and settings. Create the hero thumbnail, upload all images, attach the delivery PDF, and verify everything before telling me it's done.

---

## Create a new design

> Create a new Canva design for a [product type] in the [niche] niche. Style: [describe the look — colours, feel, references]. Show me the candidates on Spaces before you start editing. I'll pick one and tell you what text to put on it.

---

## Improve an existing listing

> Listing [number or name] isn't performing well. Pull the current title, tags, and description. Suggest improvements based on what's working in similar niches. Show me the changes side-by-side before applying. Don't touch the images unless I ask.

---

## Resume after a crash

> The last session crashed. Check GitHub commits, Canva folders, and Etsy drafts to figure out what survived. Tell me where we left off and what still needs doing. Don't redo anything that's already done.

---

## Start of day

> Good morning. Read SOUL.md and the latest standup. Check the ideas backlog for anything unchecked. Check transcripts for new files since the last digest. Tell me what's on the priority list and what you'd recommend we ship today.

---

## End of session

> We're done for today. Update CHANGELOG.md and LESSONS.md with everything we did. Commit and push all changes. Tell me exactly what shipped and what's next for tomorrow.

---

## Connect Telegram to OpenClaw

> Set up a Telegram bot that connects to the OpenClaw API. I want to send a message in Telegram and get a response from the AI. Walk me through the setup step by step — bot token, webhook, and the connection to OpenClaw.

---

## Check how listings are performing

> Pull the latest stats for my Etsy listings. Show me views, favourites, and sales for the last 7 days. Flag anything that's trending up or down. If any listing has zero views, tell me why and suggest a fix.

---

## The Golden Rule

Tell CC what you want in plain English. Show me before publishing. Verify it worked before saying it is done. CC reads the skill files for all technical details.
