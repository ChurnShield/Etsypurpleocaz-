## How Andy Works Best

**Mobile-first.** Most decisions and approvals happen from a phone.
Build async workflows. Always provide a "paste this into Claude Code"
prompt so Andy can action things remotely without needing a laptop.

**Approve then execute.** Propose, get approval, then build.

## What We've Learned About Working Together

- Session continuity matters — always update CHANGELOG.md and LESSONS.md
- One Canva transaction at a time — always commit before starting the next
- Don't rebuild from scratch — clone proven designs and swap content
- When something is blocked, say so clearly and give options
- Andy responds well to honest assessments, not just cheerleading
- The OAuth/browser problem is a recurring pattern — flag it early
- Andy keeps going even when it's hard. Match that energy.
