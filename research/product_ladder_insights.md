## Cross-Niche Summary


### Market Size & Pricing Comparison

### Saturation & Opportunity

| Niche | Saturation | Opportunity | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| barbershop bundle | Low (212) | High | Under-served market — fewer competitors, easier to rank |
| nail tech bundle | Medium (1,526) | Medium | Proven demand, moderate competition |
| lash tech bundle | Medium (1,705) | Medium | Proven demand, moderate competition |
| hair salon bundle | Medium-High (2,956) | Medium | Established market — need strong differentiation |
| tattoo bundle | High (16,050) | Requires edge | Crowded — compete on quality, bundling, or niche-down |

### Gaps & Opportunities

**1. Barbershop is wide open.**
Only 212 listings total. Lowest competition of all 5 niches. A well-designed barbershop forms/templates bundle has minimal competition.

**2. Price ladder gap across all niches.**
Most bundles cluster at the low end (under 10). Premium bundles (20+) with more components, better design, and Canva editability can capture the top of the market.

**3. Forms/paperwork bundles under-represented outside tattoo.**
Tattoo has strong forms competition (consent, intake, aftercare). Barbershop, nail tech, lash tech, and hair salon have far fewer dedicated client paperwork bundles — most results are social media or marketing templates.

**4. Cross-niche template reuse.**
Consent forms, intake forms, cancellation policies, and invoices are structurally identical across all 5 niches. Build once per form type, restyle per niche (palette + header). This is the factory model.

**5. Canva editability is a differentiator.**
Many top-favourited listings mention 'editable', 'Canva template', or 'customizable'. Buyers pay more for templates they can personalise vs static PDFs.
